# Source: EU AI Act - Annex IV Technical Documentation Requirements
**Topic:** Regulatory Compliance / Enterprise AI Governance
**Date:** May 11, 2026

Under the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), Annex IV outlines the mandatory technical documentation that providers of high-risk AI systems must produce before placing the system on the market (Article 11). This documentation is the primary evidence of conformity during a market surveillance audit. It must be retained for 10 years.

The technical documentation must contain at least the following categories of information:

1. **System Identification and General Description:**
   - Intended purpose, provider name, and version history.
   - How the AI system interacts with other hardware or software.
   - Software/Firmware versions and requirements for updates.
   - Deployment architecture (e.g., APIs, embedded hardware).

2. **Development Process and Architecture:**
   - Detailed description of the development methodology.
   - Design specifications and underlying architecture.
   - Third-Party Components: How pre-trained models or external tools were integrated or modified.
   - Datasheets describing training methodologies, data provenance, labelling procedures, and data cleaning (Article 10 data governance).

3. **Monitoring, Functioning, and Control:**
   - Performance capabilities and limitations (e.g., accuracy for specific groups, expected overall accuracy).
   - Foreseeable unintended outcomes and risks to health, safety, and fundamental rights.
   - Measures for effective human oversight (Article 14), including technical means for humans to interpret system outputs.
   - Input data specifications.

4. **Risk Management System (Article 9):**
   - Detailed documentation of the risk management system, including identification, mitigation, and residual assessment of risks throughout the lifecycle.

5. **Lifecycle Changes and Post-Market Monitoring:**
   - Description of any changes made to the system's performance throughout its lifecycle.
   - A detailed post-market monitoring plan (Article 72) to evaluate the system in the real world.

6. **Compliance Declarations:**
   - A list of applied harmonised standards or common specifications.
   - A signed EU Declaration of Conformity (Article 47).

For enterprise conversational AI directors, this means operational KPIs (intent accuracy, CSAT) are insufficient. The auditor will look for this exact Annex IV documentation structure. Absence of this documentation constitutes a conformity failure, blocking market access and risking fines up to €30M or 6% of global annual turnover.
