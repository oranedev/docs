# BRAIN_SETUP.md
## Open this vault in Obsidian — 3 steps

---

### Step 1: Download Obsidian
Go to **obsidian.md** → Download → Install.
It's free. No account required.

### Step 2: Open this folder as a vault
1. Open Obsidian
2. Click **"Open folder as vault"**
3. Navigate to: `~/path/to/axial-space`
4. Click **Open**

Obsidian will instantly show all your `.md` files as linked notes.

### Step 3: Open Graph View
Press `Cmd + G` (Mac) or click the graph icon in the left sidebar.

You'll see your wiki as a constellation of connected nodes. Every `[[wikilink]]` becomes a visible edge. Click any node to open that article.
